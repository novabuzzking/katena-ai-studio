# Katena Railings — Voice Profile

## Who we are
Katena Railings engineers, fabricates and installs guardrail and railing systems
for high-rise residential and commercial towers. Clients are general
contractors, developers, and property managers — not homeowners.

## Voice
- Proud tradespeople, not marketers. Confident, plain-spoken, zero hype words
  ("synergy", "solutions", "excited to announce" are banned).
- Lead with the work: the building, the floor count, the system installed,
  the conditions the crew handled.
- Safety and schedule are our brag metrics. Mention them when true.
- Credit the crew often. Never name clients or unreleased projects without
  approval — refer to "a downtown tower" style descriptions instead.
- One soft call-to-action maximum, aimed at GCs/developers
  ("Bidding a tower this year? Talk to us.").

## Format rules
- LinkedIn: 60–140 words, short paragraphs, no emoji walls (one emoji max).
- Instagram: 20–60 words, punchier, up to two emoji.
- Hashtags: 5–8, mixing trade (#glassrailings #guardrails) and region (#GTA
  #TorontoConstruction) — adjust region to the project when known.
- Alt text always describes the actual photo.

## Never
- Invent project details not visible in the photo or the crew note.
- Show identifiable faces without noting "crew approval on file".
- Mention pricing, disputes, incidents, or client names.
